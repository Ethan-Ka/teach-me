You're going to be great at this. Let's learn Brindle Bridge tolls together, one friendly step at a time.

Step 1: Crates

Every toll starts with the crates. Each crate costs 2 coins, and every crate beyond the 6th costs 3 coins. Example: 8 crates = 6 × 2 + 2 × 3 = 18 coins.

Step 2: Dusk and the lantern

At Dusk, the toll so far is doubled, unless the traveler's lantern counts as lit. A lit lantern only counts if the traveler has 4 crates or fewer; with more, their hands are full and the lantern is lowered. At Dawn and Day the lantern does nothing.

Quick practice

Step 3: Guilds

Millers have the toll so far halved (round down). Weavers take 4 coins off (never below 0).

Step 4: Salt

Salt adds a 5-coin fee after the guild rule, so guild discounts never touch it. No salt fee at Dawn.

Your turn!

A Miller arrives at Day with 6 crates, an unlit lantern, and salt. What's the toll after Step 4?

Hint: 6 crates is 12. Day means no doubling. The Miller halves 12 to 6, and then you add the 5-coin salt fee: 6 + 5.

Your turn!

A traveler with 5 crates and no guild arrives at Dusk with a lit lantern. What's the toll after Step 2?

Hint: 5 crates is 10 coins. Since 5 is more than 4, the lantern doesn't count, so the 10 doubles to 20.

Steps 5 and 6: Free pass and cap

If the toll so far is 4 or less, it becomes 0. And the final toll is never more than 20.

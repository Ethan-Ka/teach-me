The Brindle Bridge Tolls

Six rules, one keeper, centuries of tradition. Everything you need to know before your first shift at the bridge.

"The bridge remembers every coin." For over three hundred years the keepers of Brindle have reckoned tolls by the same six rules, handed down from keeper to apprentice by lantern-light.

The order of the reckoning

1 Crates2 Dusk3 Guild4 Salt5 Free pass6 Cap

The keeper always applies the rules in this order, each working on the toll so far.

1

Crates

2 coins per crate. Every crate beyond the 6th costs 3 coins.

2

Dusk

At Dusk the toll so far is doubled, unless the lantern counts as lit. A lit lantern counts only with 4 crates or fewer, because with more the traveler's hands are full. The lantern does nothing at Dawn or Day.

3

Guild

Millers: the toll so far is halved, rounding down. Weavers: 4 coins off, never below 0. No guild, no change.

4

Salt

A 5-coin fee for carrying salt. It's a fee, not part of the load, so the guild rule never touches it. Waived at Dawn.

5

Free pass

If the toll so far is 4 coins or less, it becomes 0.

6

Cap

The final toll is never more than 20 coins.

Did you know? The Miller's discount dates from the Great Flour Shortage, when the town council wished to keep bread cheap. The Weavers, not to be outdone, petitioned for their own.

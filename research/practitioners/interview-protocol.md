# Teacher / tutor interview protocol (~30 min)

Get consent to take notes. Don't record names in the repo; use a role ("HS math teacher, 12 yrs").

## Warm-up
1. What do you teach, to whom, and for how long?

## The tell / guide / do decision
2. Tell me about a recent time you stopped explaining and made a student try it themselves. What made you decide that was the moment?
3. Tell me about a time you let a student struggle too long, or stepped in too early. What did you learn?
4. How do you know when to take support away?

## Reading the learner
5. In the first few minutes with a new student on a topic, how do you figure out where they are?
6. What does "they think they get it but don't" look like? How do you catch it?
7. A student says "just tell me the answer." What do you do, and does it depend on anything?

## Materials
8. Show or describe a handout, worksheet or lesson that works really well. Why does it work?
9. What do bad learning materials have in common?

## Transcript review (if time)
10. Read this Claude transcript (from research/baseline). At which turn would you have done something different, and what?

## Close
11. What's one thing about teaching that took you years to learn?
